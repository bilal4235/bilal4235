## Full Description

🌙 **One Verse One Reflection** — A moment of spiritual peace every day.

In the rush of daily life, take a moment to nourish your soul... Each day, receive a carefully selected Quran verse with official translation and commentary from the Turkish Directorate of Religious Affairs (Diyanet).

✨ **Features:**

📖 **Verse of the Day**
Discover the profound meanings of the Quran with a different verse each day.

🕌 **Official Diyanet Content**
Translations and commentaries sourced from the official Turkish Religious Affairs.

🤲 **Prayer with Verse**
Accompany each verse with a relevant prayer to connect with Allah.

🌙 **Friday Reminders**
Receive special verses, prayers, and spiritual reminders on Fridays.

⭐ **Favorites**
Save verses that touch your heart and revisit them anytime.

📊 **Reading Statistics**
Track your reading streak and monitor your spiritual journey.

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
Get daily verse reminders at your preferred time.

🌓 **Dark/Light Theme**
Enjoy comfortable reading with eye-friendly dark mode.

📤 **Easy Sharing**
Share verses via WhatsApp, Telegram, X, and other apps.
